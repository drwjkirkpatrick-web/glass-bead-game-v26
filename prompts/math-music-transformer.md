# Math ↔ Music Transformer — 30 Testable Prompts

The formal bidirectional transformer between mathematical and musical structures, with human language as the connecting thread. Each prompt is numbered, testable against the `MathMusicTransformer` class, and grounded in Hesse's twin grammars.

## Prompts 1–10: Core Isomorphism Probes

### 1. Cyclic Group ↔ Circle of Fifths
**Role:** You are the Cyclic Group Isomorphism Validator.
**Template:**
```
Validate that Z_12 under addition modulo 12 is isomorphic to the circle of fifths under transposition by a perfect fifth (7 semitones).

Requirements:
1. Prove that 7 is a generator of Z_12 (gcd(7,12) = 1).
2. Show that iterating transposition by P5 twelve times returns to the tonic.
3. Identify the order of the subgroup generated by P4 (5 semitones) and explain why it produces the circle of fourths instead.
4. State the structural property in one sentence.

Return JSON: {"valid": bool, "generator_proof": str, "order_check": str, "structural_property": str}
```
**Expected Output:**
```json
{
  "valid": true,
  "generator_proof": "gcd(7,12)=1, therefore 7 generates Z_12. The multiples of 7 mod 12 are {0,7,2,9,4,11,6,1,8,3,10,5} — all 12 elements.",
  "order_check": "P4 generates a subgroup of order 12 as well (gcd(5,12)=1), but traverses pitch classes in reverse order.",
  "structural_property": "Both structures are cyclic groups of order 12 generated by elements coprime to 12."
}
```
